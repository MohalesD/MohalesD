### AI Builder Journal · live

People who build alone lose ideas between sessions. This is an idea and prompt capture tool: type, speak, or transcribe an idea, then turn it into a structured prompt and track the build. Six live AI actions, installable as a web app, and it can hand an idea directly to Ada Coach to start a discovery session.

**Proof:** I've used it near-daily since July 2026 (137+ ideas and 58+ prompts at last count). When a user reported one bug in the AI "Expand" action, I built an eval of six real notes against five deterministic checks. It found four independent defects, not one.

[Live app](https://aibuilderjournal.com)

### Ada Coach · in active development

Founders and product people skip customer discovery or run it badly. Ada is an AI coach that runs a guided discovery sprint: assumption mapping, blind-spot analysis, interview guides, and a shareable risk map, grounded in the user's own documents.

**Proof:** I found and removed a silent gate that had been switching retrieval off, then recalibrated the retrieval similarity threshold from 0.60 to 0.45 and verified the change two independent ways. Demoed to a 23-person AI product management cohort.

[Live app](https://ada-coach.vercel.app)

### Welcome Portal · sanitized demo in progress

New hires at small companies get paperwork instead of a first week. This is an employee experience portal, deployed for a luxury real estate firm: onboarding missions, a buddy system, pulse checks with escalation, an interactive org chart, and a handbook-trained AI assistant. It's now expanding from onboarding into the full employee lifecycle.

**Proof:** Six-role access model, and I cloned and rebranded it for a second company in under an hour. An adoption and usage panel is in build, so return-usage numbers with a baseline are coming next.
