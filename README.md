### AI Builder Journal · live
Solo builders lose ideas between sessions. A journal that captures notes by typing, voice, or live transcription, turns raw ideas into structured prompts, tracks builds, and hands ideas to Ada Coach for discovery.
**Proof:** I use it near-daily; at my Aug 27 count it held 137+ ideas, 58+ prompts, and 15+ tracked builds, all my own usage since March 2026. Its evals caught a real failure, a fix that was merged but never deployed.
[Live app](https://aibuilderjournal.com)

### Ada Coach · in development
Early-stage founders and product people skip customer discovery or run it badly. An AI coach that walks them through a discovery sprint using frameworks like Jobs to Be Done, assumption mapping, and The Mom Test, grounded by retrieval.
**Proof:** a retrieval similarity threshold set at 0.45 and verified two independent ways, plus a silent failure I found and removed where retrieval was quietly switching itself off. Next measured: latency and model-routing cost per session. Demoed to a 12-week AI PM cohort of about 23 peers.
[Live app](https://ada-coach.vercel.app)

### Welcome Portal · in production, public demo in progress
A luxury real estate firm's onboarding was paperwork and email chains. A branded portal that turns a new hire's first weeks into a guided experience, then grows into a directory, org chart, and employee lifecycle hub through offboarding.
**Proof:** the same product was cloned and rebranded for a prospect in under one hour, against a 30 to 45 minute target for future deployments. A sanitized demo with synthetic people is coming.
